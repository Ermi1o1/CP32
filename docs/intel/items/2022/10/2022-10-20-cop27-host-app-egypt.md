Egypt's COP27 application was published by its Ministry of Communications and Information Technology. It extends the host-ministry publishing pattern back to 2022.

**So what for us:** Strengthens the reality check's precedent base: African and Middle Eastern hosts have built their own COP apps through a government ICT ministry. The likely Ethiopian counterpart is MinT.
