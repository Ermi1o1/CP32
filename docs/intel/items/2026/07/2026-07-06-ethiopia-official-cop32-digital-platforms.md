Fana reported on 6 July 2026 that Ethiopia's COP32 preparations now include what it describes as the launch of the conference's official website and digital communication platforms, intended to carry verified information and updates. The same report cites "the official COP32 Ethiopia platform" as its own source for a bilateral meeting between Ethiopia's COP32 President-Designate and his COP30 counterpart. Nothing in the report describes a mobile application, a delegate platform, or a procurement. What was actually launched, and by whom, is not established.

**So what for us:** Goes directly at R1 and at D1's premise that no official COP32 platform had been announced as of 2026-10-01. On the evidence so far this looks like a website plus communications channels rather than an app, which would leave the app niche open - but D1 cannot be relied on until the primary source is read.
