## Pipeline findings (no change applied)

**The filter is working.** The `match: '\bCOP[\s\-_]?3[0-9]\b'` regex on the
two `apple_search` sources already prevents the noise seen in `ALERTS.md`; the
comment in `sources.yml` records that the first live run alerted on 31
unrelated apps, which is what the backlog was. Tier-1 counts since: 2026-11-10
zero, 11-11 one, 11-12 two, 11-13 zero, 11-14 zero. No filter change is needed
and none is proposed.

**What is actually wrong:**

1. **`tier_floor: 1` on both app-store sources forces every matching listing to
   Tier 1**, including COP16, COP27 and COP31 listings that are benchmark
   material, not COP32 alerts. All six app listings filed today were retiered to
   4 by hand. Suggest the floor fire only on a COP32/ኮፕ32 subject hit, with
   other COP numbers landing at Tier 4.
2. **The watched COP32 host domain may be the wrong one.** `sources.yml` watches
   `cop32ethiopia.org`, marked `trust: official, verified: 2026-11-09`, which
   serves a WordPress maintenance page. `cop32ethiopia.et` and `cop32.gov.et`
   are both candidates and neither is watched. **Not changed in this review** —
   the open question above has to be answered first, and silently repointing a
   watcher marked "official" at an unverified domain would make the same mistake
   in the other direction. Recommended once settled: watch the confirmed
   official host, keep the others under watch at `trust: unknown`, and flag any
   that turns out not to be ours.
3. **`unfccc.int/cop32` is `enabled: false, verified: unverified`.** With COP31
   under way and the COP32 Presidency launched, that page is now worth enabling.
4. **No procurement source.** Nothing watches e-GP or Ethiopian tender notices,
   which is where a COP32 app contract would surface first. This is the single
   highest-value source still missing.
5. **The alert loop has no closing step.** A Tier-1 item sat 131 days because
   `ALERTS.md` is append-only and nothing forces triage. Suggest the daily digest
   report the count of open Tier-1 alerts older than seven days.
