## A host-country guide for COP32, built and working

**What it is.** A phone app for everyone at COP32 who is not in the negotiating rooms: visitors, residents, journalists, volunteers and people following from afar. Programme, venue map, practical guides, plain-language explainers.

**What it is not.** It is not the UN's delegate platform, and it is not official. It is independent, says so on its first screen, and uses no government, UN or official COP32 emblem.

### Why now
Recent conferences have each had a host-country app beside the UN's delegate platform. For COP31 it appeared about a week before the opening. This one exists a year ahead, so it can be tested, corrected and made yours with time to spare.

### What is different about it
| | |
|---|---|
| **Works without a connection** | The whole programme, the map, the guides and search are stored on the phone at install. Around a crowded venue the network fails first; this does not depend on it. |
| **Private by design** | No account. Saved sessions never leave the phone. No advertising and no tracking software. It asks for no access to location, contacts, camera, microphone or storage. ★ |
| **Small** | About 25 MB to install ★ — a fraction of a typical event app — so it suits modest phones and expensive mobile data. |
| **Honest** | Where a fact is not confirmed it says "to be confirmed". No AI assistant that may be wrong; no invented news, numbers or dates. A "Sources and status" page lists what is reported, what is illustrative and what is unknown. |
| **Accessible** | Light and dark themes, text up to 200%, large touch targets, colour contrast checked against WCAG 2.2 AA on every build. |
| **Ready to wear your identity** | Name, mark and colours come from one setting and can be changed the same day. |

### What it needs from the host
1. **The programme and venue plan**, in any structured form, when they exist.
2. **Agreement on who may authorise alerts** — until then, alerts stay switched off.
3. **A decision on name and marks.**

### What is honest to say about its state
It is a demonstration build. The sessions and speakers shown are examples. The Amharic text is an unreviewed draft. It has not had a security audit or testing with users, and it is not in any app store. These are the next steps, and they are better done with the host than without.
