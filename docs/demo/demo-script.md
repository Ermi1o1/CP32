# Demo script — COP32 guide app (draft 3)

**Written:** 2026-11-15; revised 2026-11-16 for app version 1.0.0 (new Home, raised blocks, floating tab bar, explainer pages, no network permission; D78–D80). **Status: draft for the founder. Not rehearsed, and the app has not yet been verified on a phone at this version** — run the phone checklist (`docs/operations/device-checklist.md`) before using this in front of anyone.
**Length:** 8 minutes of showing, then questions. **Audience:** senior officials and their digital or communications leads.
**Positioning (D70):** a host-country guide for COP32, built independently and offered for adoption. Not a competing bid. Not official.

**The founder set these three aside as conditions on 2026-11-16 (D80). They stay here as risks to walk in knowing about; none is resolved.**
1. The app has passed the phone checklist on the phone you will hold.
2. You know **which office owns COP32 digital delivery** and whether an app is already planned or contracted (reality check, re-check #3: unknown; the procurement portal has not been searched).
3. Contributor IP assignments are signed (D39, D41, D72) — ownership is the first thing an adoption conversation turns on.

---

## Before you walk in
- Phone charged, brightness up, notifications silenced, the app already opened once.
- Settings → Demonstration → **Conference-day preview: off.** Brand: COP32. Appearance: Light.
- Save nothing in advance; you will save a session live.
- Know how to turn on airplane mode in one swipe.

## The opening (30 seconds) — say this before showing the screen
> "Every recent climate conference has had two apps: the UN's platform for delegates, and a host-country app for everyone else — visitors, the public, the press, volunteers. Türkiye launched theirs a week before COP31. We have built a working version of that second app for Addis Ababa, a year early. It is independent — it is not official, and it does not pretend to be. We would like to show you what it does, and then ask you three things."

## The six screens

| # | Screen | Do | Say | Time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Home** | Open the app. Let it sit for two seconds. Scroll once. | "This is what a visitor sees. What the guide is, a search field, a few highlights to swipe through, the first sessions. On the green card at the top: *saved offline* — remember that." Point to the line "Independent guide · not an official COP32 app". "That line stays until you tell us otherwise." | 1:00 |
| 2 | **Programme** | Tap Programme. Tap Day 2, then "Open to public". | "The programme, one card per session. A visitor's first question is 'what can I actually attend?' — one tap. Every session says who may enter, or says plainly that it is not yet confirmed. These sessions are examples; the real programme is one of our three asks." | 1:00 |
| 3 | **Session** | Open a session. Tap **Save to my agenda**. | "Time, place, who is speaking. Saving needs no account — nothing leaves the phone. No sign-up, no tracking, no advertising. This build does not even hold the permission to use the network; you can check that in the phone's settings. 'Share this session' hands the details to the phone's own share sheet; the app itself sends nothing." | 1:00 |
| 4 | **Map** | Tap Map. Select Hall B. Tap "Step-free". | "A venue plan — this one is a schematic, because the venue is not announced. With your floor plans, this becomes real wayfinding. Step-free routes are one tap." | 0:45 |
| 5 | **Offline** — the centre of the demo | Swipe down, **airplane mode on**. Close the app fully. Reopen it. Open Programme, open a session not opened before, open Search and type "water". | "Now the phone has no connection. Everything still works: the whole programme, the map, the guides, search. In November, around a venue with tens of thousands of phones, the network will be the first thing to fail. This app does not need it." Pause. Let them try. | 1:30 |
| 6 | **Your identity** | Profile → Demonstration → Brand identity → "Your Event". Go back to Home. Then switch back. | "Name, mark and colours come from one setting. If this becomes yours, it wears your identity the same day — nothing is rebuilt." | 0:45 |

Optional seventh, if there is interest (1:00): Profile → Demonstration → **Conference-day preview**. "This is the same app on a conference day: the next session, what is live, what has ended." Turn it off again afterwards.

## Then: what it deliberately does not do (30 seconds)
> "It has no AI assistant that might give a wrong answer about a gate or an emergency. It shows no phone numbers we have not confirmed with you. It invents no news. Where it does not know something, it says 'to be confirmed'."

## The honest answer to "Is this official?"
> "No. It is independent. It uses no government, UN or official COP32 emblem, and it says it is unofficial on the first screen, on the home page and in About. We named it for the event it is about; if you prefer another name, it changes in a minute. It becomes official only if you decide so, in writing."

If asked **"We already have a team / a vendor working on this"** — do not defend the app. Say:
> "Then we are glad to hear it, and we are not here to compete with them. What we have is a working reference: offline-first, private by design, small enough for any phone. If any of it is useful to your team — the approach, the code, or us as an extra pair of hands — that is the conversation we would like to have."

If asked **"Who are you and who owns this?"** — answer plainly and briefly; say what is and is not yet in place. Do not claim a registration, a partnership or an endorsement that does not exist.

If asked **"Does it work in Amharic?"**
> "The structure is ready for Amharic and other languages, and a draft is in the app. A proper Amharic edition needs native-speaking editors — we would want to do that with you, not guess at it."

## The three asks
1. **Programme data.** A structured export of the official programme and venue plan when they exist — even a spreadsheet. Without it the app shows examples.
2. **Authorised alerts.** Who may issue a schedule change or a safety notice, and how we verify it. Until that is agreed, alerts stay switched off.
3. **Identity.** Whether, and on what terms, the app may carry the official name and marks — or should stay under its own.

Close:
> "We are not asking for a decision today. We are asking who the right person is for each of those three."

## What not to say
- Do not give dates for COP32, attendance figures or a venue. They are not confirmed.
- Do not say the app is "ready to launch". It is a demonstration build: unreviewed Amharic, example content, no security audit, no store listing.
- Do not say it has been tested by users or on a range of devices. It has not.
- Do not criticise the COP31 app, and never show screenshots of it.
- Do not say "nobody has started". An official COP32 website and channels exist.

## After the meeting
Write down, the same day: who was in the room, which of the three asks got an answer and from whom, and anything said about an existing app or vendor. Record it in `docs/decisions/log.md`.
